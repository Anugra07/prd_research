# Phase 0 - Business / Market Interview

For business and full mode. Ask 3-6, picked for what you saw in the product. Do not start research until answered. If the user says "go without it", proceed but mark dependent recommendations "assumption-based - validate before acting".

## The bank

Pick the most load-bearing 3-6. Reword to fit the product.

### Product and customer (almost always ask)
1. **Who is it for.** Who is the primary user/buyer today, and what job are they hiring it to do? Is the buyer the user?
2. **Current traction.** Roughly how many users/customers and what revenue (or pre-revenue)? Growth rate if known?

### Money
3. **Monetization.** How do you make money today (or plan to)? Pricing/packaging, and is it per-seat, usage, flat, freemium?
4. **Unit economics, if known.** Rough CAC, payback, retention/churn, gross margin? "Don't know" is a valid answer and itself a finding.

### Market and competition
5. **Competition.** Who do you lose deals to, and who do prospects compare you against (including "they build it themselves" or "they do nothing")?
6. **Differentiator.** In one sentence, why do your best customers choose you over the alternative?

### Goals
7. **Where you want to be.** What does "next level" mean concretely in 6-18 months - revenue, segment, market position?
8. **Expansion appetite.** Any expansion directions you're already considering (new segment, geography, product line, platform)? Any you've ruled out?

### Constraints / non-negotiables
9. **Constraints.** Budget, team size/skills, compliance (SOC2/HIPAA/GDPR), or strategic non-negotiables (e.g. "must stay self-serve", "can't go enterprise yet")?
10. **Biggest worry.** What keeps you up at night about the business - the thing you most want this analysis to pressure-test?

## How to use the answers

- **Who it's for + differentiator** anchor the UVP analysis (Phase B3). If the user's stated differentiator isn't visible in the product or market, surface that gap honestly.
- **Traction + monetization + unit economics** set which growth levers are realistic (Phase B4) and weight business-recommendation impact. Missing unit economics -> recommend instrumenting them as a prerequisite.
- **Competition** seeds the positioning research (Phase B2). Always include the "do nothing / build in-house" alternative.
- **Goals + expansion appetite** scope Phase B5 and become the tie-breakers when recommendations score equally.
- **Constraints + biggest worry** are hard filters and priority weights, exactly as in the technical interview.

## Vague answers

- "Everyone" for the customer -> push once for the sharpest segment that already gets the most value; if none, flag "no clear ICP" as a finding.
- "We're better/cheaper/faster" for the differentiator -> ask for the proof a skeptical buyer would demand.
- "We don't track that" for metrics -> proceed, and make "instrument the funnel and unit economics" an early recommendation.
